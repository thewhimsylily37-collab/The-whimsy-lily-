Our Story

Welcome to The Whimsy Lily

Hi there 🌷

A tiny corner of the internet, made with love.

Welcome to The Whimsy Lily, a tiny corner of the internet where every handmade piece is created with love, patience, and a little bit of magic. What started as a love for crocheting has grown into a place where I get to turn simple threads, yarn, and ideas into keepsakes that make people smile.

From cute keychains to cozy crochet creations and custom orders, each item is carefully handmade with attention to every little detail. I truly believe that the smallest gifts often hold the biggest memories, and I hope my creations become a tiny part of yours.

Thank you for stopping by and supporting a small dream from Assam. Your love, kind words, and every single order mean more than you know. I can't wait to create something special just for you! 💗

Meet the Maker

Hi, I'm Rishita.

I'm the owner of The Whimsy Lily. It started when I began thinking about my mom's passion for crochet. She has been making art for as long as I can remember, and I think a lot of my own creative enthusiasm comes from her.

Because of her, our house has always felt like a home. There is a certain warmth in the things she makes — something that has made our home more beautiful in a way that no expensive decoration ever could. I have grown up surrounded by handmade art, creativity, and the love that goes into making something with your own hands.

I had always wanted to start something of my own, and at the same time, I wanted my mom's talent and passion to reach more people. So, one fine morning, I decided to start The Whimsy Lily.

It is my little attempt to take something that has brought so much warmth to our home and share it with others. I hope that our creations find their way into as many homes as possible and bring a little bit of the same warmth, love, and happiness with them.
